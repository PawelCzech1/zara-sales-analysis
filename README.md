# zara-sales-analysis# **ZARA Sales and Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis – Excel Dashboard**

## **Project Description**

The goal of this project was to perform an exploratory analysis of ZARA’s sales data to identify sales trends, seasonality patterns, and factors influencing revenue. The project focused on extracting practical insights that support business decision‑making.

## **Tools and Techniques Used**

- **Power Query** – data cleaning and transformation (ETL)  
- **Excel** – main tool for analysis and visualization  
- **Pivot tables and pivot charts** – data aggregation and creation of interactive visualizations  
- **Slicers** – filtering results by product type  
- **Charts:**  
  - KPI tiles (total revenue, total sales volume, number of product categories)  
  - Line chart – comparison of seasonal vs. non‑seasonal product sales over time  
  - Bar chart – sales of promotional vs. non‑promotional products by category  
  - Combo chart – revenue by category and percentage share in total revenue  
- **Dashboard UX** – two thematic pages, KPI tiles at the top, slicer placed in a visible area for user convenience  

## **Key Insights**

- **Revenue Structure**  
  - Total revenue amounts to **$28.01M**, generated from **31,473 transactions**.  
  - The average revenue per transaction is approximately **$890**.

- **Revenue Concentration by Category**  
  - The **Jackets** category accounts for approximately **60–65%** of total revenue.  
  - Revenue from this category is **more than five times higher** than the second‑largest category.  
  - The remaining four categories together generate **35–40%** of revenue.  
  - Conclusion: revenue is highly concentrated in one dominant category.

- **Volume Analysis – Best‑Selling Products**  
  - The **Top 10 products** each reach sales volumes between **2,000 and 12,000 units**.

## **Recommendations**

- The priority should be further optimization of the **Jackets** category, as it generates the largest share of revenue and has the strongest impact on overall performance.  
- Strengthening the remaining categories is recommended to reduce dependency on a single segment and improve revenue stability.  
- The best‑selling products (Top 10) show strong potential — increasing their availability could quickly boost sales volume.  
- Conducting a seasonality analysis is recommended, as the dominance of one category may indicate demand fluctuations across different periods.  
- Implementing ongoing KPI monitoring (category share, volume, average revenue) is advisable, as regular tracking of trends enables faster response to changes in sales performance.
